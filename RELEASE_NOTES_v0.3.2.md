# Teddy Mail v0.3.2

Inbox management upgrades.

## What's new
- **Reply All** in MailReader (button + Shift+R) : pre-fills To with the sender, CC with all original recipients minus your accounts.
- **Saved view CRUD** : sidebar entries for saved searches now have hover ✎ rename and ✕ delete.
- **Mock new-mail simulator** : every 5 minutes, a synthetic mail arrives in the inbox (different senders, categories). Plays the notify sound, shows toast, native browser notification on click. Respects your quiet hours pref. Will be replaced by real IMAP IDLE in V0.4.
- **Labels CRUD** : 🏷 button in MailReader header opens a picker with checkboxes for existing labels and an inline "create new label" input. Labels show as colored chips on thread cards in the list.
